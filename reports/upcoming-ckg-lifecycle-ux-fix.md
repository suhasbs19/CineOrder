## 1. Executive Summary & Root Cause

### Problem Description
For upcoming / unreleased titles (such as *Supergirl: Woman of Tomorrow* with 0 required prerequisites), the Narrative Knowledge Graph Explorer previously displayed:
- `"Ready to Watch — Standalone Entry Point"` (with a green checkmark)
- `"Story Readiness 100%"`
- Active `"Mark Watched"` action buttons in `MovieDetailPage`, `Timeline`, and `StoryGraphNodeHierarchy`

This conflated **Story Prerequisite Readiness** (which is 100% when 0 required prerequisites exist) with **Release Availability** (which is false for an upcoming, unreleased movie), confusing users into thinking the unreleased title was available to stream or watch today.

### Root Cause Analysis
1. **Entry Point Banner Coupling in `PreparationGuide.tsx`**:
   The standalone entry point banner unconditionally rendered `"Ready to Watch — Standalone Entry Point"` whenever `graphResult.isEntryPoint === true`, regardless of whether `lifecycleCategory === 'UPCOMING'`.
2. **Ambiguous Readiness Metric Label in `PreparationGuide.tsx`**:
   The readiness metric was labeled as `"Story Readiness"`, implying the title itself was ready for viewing rather than describing prerequisite story preparation.
3. **Active Watch Toggle for Unreleased Titles**:
   `MovieDetailPage.tsx`, `Timeline.tsx`, `StoryGraphNodeHierarchy.tsx`, and `watchStore.ts` did not guard against marking unreleased titles as watched.
4. **Timeline Warnings Passthrough**:
   `graphResult.timelineWarnings` generated by the graph traversal engine (`'✅ Ready to Watch. This title is an excellent entry point.'`) were displayed verbatim without adapting the presentation for upcoming pre-release modes.

## 2. Universal Lifecycle Logic Implemented

Zero title-specific hacks were added (no `if (title.id === 'dc-supergirl')`). The logic is 100% universal across all franchises and titles based on the authoritative lifecycle state:

### A. Separation of Story Readiness vs Release Availability
- **Story Preparation Readiness**: Evaluates prerequisite completion (`storyReadinessPercentage`). For standalone titles with 0 required prerequisites, this remains 100%.
- **Release Availability**: Evaluates theatrical and streaming premiere status (`lifecycleCategory === 'UPCOMING'`, `isTheatricallyUpcoming(content)`).

### B. Adaptive Entry Point Banner ([`src/components/ui/PreparationGuide.tsx`](file:///c:/web/src/components/ui/PreparationGuide.tsx))
- **When `isUpcoming` is `true`**:
  - Banner Title: `"Upcoming — Story Preparation Available"` (with Sparkles icon & amber badge).
  - Banner Description: `"No previous movies are required before this upcoming title. Recommended background viewing provides optional character and world context before release."`
  - Readiness Label: `"Story Preparation Readiness: 100%"`.
- **When `isUpcoming` is `false`**:
  - Banner Title: `"Ready to Watch — Standalone Entry Point"` (with CheckCircle icon & emerald badge).
  - Banner Description: `"No previous movies are required to understand the main feature story. Recommended titles provide additional character and world context."`

### C. Disabled Watch Action for Unreleased Titles
- In [`src/pages/MovieDetailPage.tsx`](file:///c:/web/src/pages/MovieDetailPage.tsx), [`src/components/ui/PreparationGuide.tsx`](file:///c:/web/src/components/ui/PreparationGuide.tsx), [`src/components/ui/StoryGraphNodeHierarchy.tsx`](file:///c:/web/src/components/ui/StoryGraphNodeHierarchy.tsx), and [`src/components/ui/Timeline.tsx`](file:///c:/web/src/components/ui/Timeline.tsx):
  - Unreleased titles render a disabled `<Button variant="secondary" disabled>Not Yet Released</Button>`.
- In [`src/store/watchStore.ts`](file:///c:/web/src/store/watchStore.ts):
  - `markWatched` and `toggleWatched` reject marking unreleased titles as watched with a defensive console warning.
